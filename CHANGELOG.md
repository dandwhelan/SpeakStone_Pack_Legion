# SpeakStone 4.1.2

Voices similar to the in-game characters. Unofficial; not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

Version 4.1.2 covers the base add-ons, every retail sound pack and every WoW Forever pack.

## What's new

- About 1,300 more lines re-recorded after player and owner reports, mostly Kul Tiran, Tauren, Goblin and Dracthyr characters.
- 51 characters who had lines waiting without a voice now speak, including Breem, Ba'kel, Katy Stampwhistle, One-Eyed Thak and the Mnemis keepers in Bastion.
- Nine characters the game shows as either a man or a woman now have a voice for each, and you hear the one that matches the character in front of you (for example Alliance Worker, Dark Iron Grenadier, Forsaken Dreadmage, Zandalari Vagrant and Tortollan Seeker).
- Colonel Troteman (Redridge Mountains) has a new voice that fits him.
- Goblin voices were shared out more evenly, so fewer goblins sound alike.
- Quest titles, character names, books and greetings in the Audio Library updated to match the new lines.
