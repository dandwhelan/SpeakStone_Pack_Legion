# SpeakStone voice pack: Legion

Legion quests, voiced for SpeakStone, with voices similar to the in-game characters. For World of Warcraft (retail).

Unofficial; not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

Website: https://speakstone.beanw.co.uk

**This pack is audio only and needs [SpeakStone](https://github.com/dandwhelan/SpeakStone_Main).** On its own it does nothing.

![Welcome tutorial](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dandwhelan/SpeakStone_Main/main/docs/screenshots/01_welcome_tutorial.png)

## What it does

SpeakStone reads World of Warcraft out loud. Open a quest, talk to an NPC or read a book, and you hear it in a voice
similar to the in-game character who says it, not one generic narrator. The same NPC keeps the same voice in their
quests, their greetings and their books. This pack holds the lines for: **Legion quests**.

- **Choose how it reads:** full narration, story only or manual; a compact speech bar, subtitles or audio only; a
  portrait or a moving 3D head of the speaker.
- **Walk away and keep listening:** the speech bar carries on after you leave the NPC, and the next line waits its turn.
- **Read-along:** the words being spoken are highlighted as the voice reads them. **Pause and Resume** pick up from where you stopped.
- **Controller and TV support**, **Dialogue UI support**, **dyslexia-friendly fonts** and three themes.
- **The SpeakStone Guide:** browse and play every voiced quest, greeting and book in game.

## How it works

1. **[SpeakStone](https://github.com/dandwhelan/SpeakStone_Main) is the player.** It holds the code, the settings, the Guide and the text, but no audio.
2. **The voice packs hold the audio** (one per expansion, plus Chatter Part 1 and 2 for greetings and books). When the game loads, this pack registers its lines with the base
   add-on. A line whose pack is missing is simply silent.
3. **When a quest, greeting or book opens**, SpeakStone looks the line up and plays it if a pack has it. Long lines are
   stored in parts of about 10 to 15 seconds, cut in natural pauses, so Pause and Resume carry on from where you stopped.
4. **Lines with no voice yet are saved as you play.** Export them from the minimap button (right-click) and paste them
   on the [website](https://speakstone.beanw.co.uk), or let the free SpeakStone Worker app send them. New lines are
   usually voiced by the next day and arrive in the packs with their next update.

Full details, commands and the rest of the screenshots: [SpeakStone README](https://github.com/dandwhelan/SpeakStone_Main#readme).

## Install

1. [SpeakStone](https://github.com/dandwhelan/SpeakStone_Main) (the base add-on, required).
2. The packs for the content you play. Install both Chatter parts: without them nearly every greeting and book is silent.

The tutorial and the Guide's Voice packs tab list any pack you are missing.
